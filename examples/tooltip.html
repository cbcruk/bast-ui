Tooltip

Shows on hover (after a delay) or on focus (immediately), and hides on pointer leave (after close-delay), blur, or Escape. The trigger gets aria-describedby while open; the popup is role="tooltip". Hovering the tooltip itself keeps it open. Positioning reuses the same side / align / side-offset config as Popover and Menu.

Top Anchored above the trigger, flipping if there's no room. Right This one opens to the right. Bottom And this one opens below.
